Lasagna Recipe

Fresh Lasagna

Lasagna has allways been one of my favorite dishes.

My personal recipe is the following. Remeber that recipes are just a guideline and you can allways switch things up by adding or removing ingredients. Happy cooking!

Ingredients

White Sauce

  • 2 Red Onions
  • 2 Carrots
  • 2 Zucchini
  • 1 Celery
  • Gouda
  • 200g Creme Fraiche
  • Salt and Pepper

Red Sauce

  • 2-3 Garlic
  • 500ml Tomatoes
  • Mushed Tomatoes
  • Dried Tomatoes

Other

  • Lasagna Pasta
  • 2 Vegan Cheese
  • Grated Vegan Cheese

Steps

  1. Chop onions, carrots, zucchinis, celery
  2. frie in pan with olive oil for 10min or so
  3. Add Creme Fraiche and 100g grated Cheese
  4. Add salt and pepper
  5. preheat oven to 180°C circulating
  6. Chop garlic
  7. In a small pot frie garlic with olive oil
  8. Add dried Tomatoes
  9. Add other Tomatoes
  10. Cover casserole dish in olive oil
  11. First layer: white sauce
  12. Second layer: pasta
  13. Third layer: red sauce
  14. Repeat and end with white sauce
  15. Add grated vegan cheese, vegan cheese and oregano
  16. Bake in oven for 30min
