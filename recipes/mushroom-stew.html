Mushroom Stew Recipe

Mushroom Stew

Ingredients

  • Fresh Mushrooms
  • 2 Onions
  • Sherry
  • 400ml Soy Cream
  • Salt, Pepper, Paprika Spice

Steps

  1. Chop onions
  2. Frie onions in olive oil
  3. Chop mushrooms
  4. Frie mushrooms for at least 20min
  5. When mushrooms are ready add Sherry
  6. After 5min add soy cream
  7. Add spices
